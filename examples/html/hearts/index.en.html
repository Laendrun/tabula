Overview

A classic trick-taking game where you try not to win certain cards. Every heart costs you a point and the queen of spades costs a lot, unless you're bold enough to take them all.

Components

  • 1x deck — standard 52 cards, no jokers
  • 1x score pad and a pencil

Our 2 of diamonds is bent: use it as the removed card for 3 players.

Goal

Have the fewest points when someone reaches 100.

Setup

  1. 3pRemove the 2 of diamonds from the deck.
  2. 5pRemove the 2 of diamonds and the 2 of clubs from the deck.
  3. Deal all cards: 3p174p135p10 cards each.

4pPassing

Before each hand, every player passes 3 cards: left, right, across, then no pass. Repeat.

3,5pPassing

Before each hand, every player passes 3 cards: left, right, then no pass.

Play

Follow suit if you can; otherwise play any card.

  • The player holding the 3-4p25p3 of clubs leads the first trick.
  • On the first trick you may not play a heart or the queen of spades, unless you have nothing else.
  • Hearts can't be led until hearts are broken.
  • The highest card of the led suit wins the trick; its winner leads next.

Scoring

  • Each heart: 1 point. Queen of spades: 13 points.
  • +omnibusThe jack of diamonds: -10 points.
  • If you take all hearts and the queen of spades, you shoot the moon: you score 0 and everyone else scores 26.

Write each hand's score on the score pad.

The game ends when a player reaches 100 points. Lowest score wins.

Glossary

Trick

One card from each player; the highest card of the led suit takes them all.

Hearts are broken

A heart has been discarded on an earlier trick, so hearts may now be led.

Shoot the moon

Taking every heart plus the queen of spades in a single hand.
