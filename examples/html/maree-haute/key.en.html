Play

Your turn

Always end your turn with 2p73p6*5 cards in hand. If you have more, throw cards away until the count is right.

Always going up

In a boat, each card must have a higher rank than the card under it. You can never slip in a lower card or take one out, except when a Tide card says so.

◆ A boat of hearts

On a boat 3♥ 5♥, you can lay the 9♥, but not the 4♥ or the 6♠.

2pWith two players

With two players, a card may also have the same rank as the card under it.

A boat of five cards or more is safe: Tide cards no longer touch it.

End of the game

Full quay

If you have laid all the boats allowed and each has at least four cards, call "Full quay!" at the end of your turn. The game ends at once and you score 10 more points.
