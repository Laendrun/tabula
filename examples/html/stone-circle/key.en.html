Play

  • A stone that touches the large rock is burnt: remove it.

Scoring

Closest stone to the rock that is still inside the circle: 3 points. Every other stone inside the circle: 1 point.

+teamsTeam play

  • Team-mates' stones add up; scores are kept per team.
